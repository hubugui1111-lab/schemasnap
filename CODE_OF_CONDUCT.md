# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for different viewpoints, accepting
responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized
language or attention, trolling, insults, harassment, and publishing another person's private information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban
contributors whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Report conduct issues
privately through the repository's security advisory channel. Maintainers will respect the reporter's
privacy and apply consequences proportionate to impact and context.
