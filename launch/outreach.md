# Personalized outreach template

Use only for people who have publicly written about data contracts, schema drift, Polars, Arrow, or
DuckDB. Mention their specific work; never send the unchanged template in bulk.

> Hi {name} — your {specific post/project} helped me think about {specific idea}. I built SchemaSnap,
> a small local “Git diff for data” CLI. It snapshots CSV/Parquet/Arrow/DuckDB results without raw
> strings or category labels, then reports deterministic contract drift in CI. The deliberate blind
> spot is equal-frequency label replacement. If that intersects your work, I would value a critical
> look at {one relevant file/rule}; no expectation to star or share it.

Checklist before sending:

- The v0.1.0 link and named file work without authentication.
- The opening sentence is specific and truthful.
- The recipient has invited project/research discussion.
- The ask is one bounded feedback question, not a star request.
- No tracking link, automation, follow-up sequence, or scraped private address is used.
